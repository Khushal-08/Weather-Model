Known Operational Limitations & Failure Modes

report_problem
Unregistered Biomass & Debris Flaring

Sudden unpredicted open biomass burning or municipal dump fire outside the 15km satellite raster buffer may produce localized PM2.5 under-predictions up to 2 hours until nearest ground station picks up anomalous plume.

cloud
Monsoon Optical Satellite Attenuation

Persistent overcast stratus cloud decks during active southwest monsoon (July–August) blind optical AOD sensors. The ensemble autonomously switches weights to ground BAM telemetry and numerical advection.

apartment
Street-Canyon Microscale Turbulence

The 500m ward-grid cannot resolve building-scale eddy vortexes inside narrow urban street canyons (e.g., Lower Parel, Dadar TT). Spatial estimates reflect aggregated ward ambient air, not individual lane micro-climates.

Failure Alert: Model pipeline flags confidence uncertainty if sensor ping latency exceeds 45 minutes.
gavel

Statutory Decision Guardrails & Responsible Use

policy Decision-Support, Not Automated Enforcement

AQIS forecasts serve as statutory advisory triggers to schedule ward inspection patrols, mist-cannon deployment, and traffic diversion. Physical on-site ground verification by MCGM field officers is legally mandated prior to issuing punitive stop-work notices under the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1981.

science Statistical Proxy, Not Chemical Apportionment

Feature importance vectors indicate spatiotemporal correlation likelihood and do not substitute for lab-grade Chemical Mass Balance (CMB) or Positive Matrix Factorization (PMF) speciation analyses in court appeals.
